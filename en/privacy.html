Publisher & contact

Turnlet is provided by Bayram Baş. For questions about the game, support or privacy, contact bayrambas255@icloud.com.

Information in the game

The game stores puzzle and daily progress, your active and saved campaign attempts, undo history, hint use and preferences in the app’s storage on your device. Local backup and recovery files also stay there. A separate local continuation record binds a daily puzzle to the day it was started for free, so it can be continued after the date changes. The game does not send these saves to a server. It contains no account system, advertising, tracking or analytics SDK.

Purchases through Apple

Workshop Collection is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Apple’s App Store services handle payment and purchase restoration. Apple processes account, payment and transaction information under its own policies. See App Store & Privacy.

The app evaluates Apple’s verified transaction and access information on the device to determine access to content. It does not send this information to a server operated by us; we do not ask for card details or your Apple Account password for support. Purchase ownership is separate from your progress backup. Purchases and restoration require a connection to Apple’s services.

Backups & sharing

Export backup hands a JSON file containing progress and preferences to the iOS file interface. The location you select may be on your device, iCloud or another file provider; that service’s own terms apply. There is no automatic in-game cloud sync. Purchase ownership and the local daily-continuation record are not included in this file. Purchases are restored separately through Apple on another device.

Share result hands the Daily Machine date and whether hints were used to the iOS share menu as result text. You choose its destination. Imported backups are read on your device.

Resetting & deleting

Settings → Reset progress starts active progress, saved campaign attempts and the collection over while preserving preferences. It does not cancel your Apple purchase or delete Apple’s transaction history. The previous save is archived locally for recovery. Importing a backup or restoring an earlier save also preserves the save being replaced.

Resetting does not remove every local file. Deleting the app and its data removes its local saves and recovery files. Offloading the app is not the same operation. Manage any exported copies separately in the location you selected. Operating-system and device backups depend on your own settings.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your sender email address, message and any attachments you choose to include to understand and respond to your request. The game does not send these automatically. Please avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information. You can use the same address to request information about, or deletion of, the support messages you sent.

This website

The hosting provider for this static support website is GitHub Pages. We have added no analytics, advertising, cookies, external fonts or executable scripts to the site.

Visiting the website is separate from playing the offline game. GitHub states that it logs and stores Pages visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement. These describe GitHub’s processing; they do not mean that the game sends your saves to GitHub.

Updates
