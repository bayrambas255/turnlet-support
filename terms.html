Content & progression

The first 20 campaign puzzles, hints and undo are free. Completing campaign puzzle 10 opens today’s Daily Machine for free. The daily selection comes from a rotating set of 60 puzzles. You can continue an unfinished daily attempt started for free after its date changes while its matching records remain on your device.

Workshop Collection is an optional purchase providing access to the remaining 100 campaign puzzles and all 60 puzzles in the Daily Library without the one-a-day wait. Each subsequent workshop opens after you complete 16 of the previous workshop’s 20 puzzles. Buying does not skip this progression requirement, complete puzzles or build workshop machines; machines are earned by playing.

Purchases & restoration

Workshop Collection is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. Check the local price shown in the app and the payment information on Apple’s purchase confirmation screen before confirming. Apple handles payment. Purchases and restoration require a connection to Apple’s services and a valid Apple Account.

To restore a previous purchase, use the Apple Account you purchased with and choose Settings → Restore purchases. Access depends on a purchase entitlement verified by Apple; a refunded or revoked entitlement may no longer provide access. Restoring purchases does not restore game progress. Completions, unfinished attempts and earned machines are kept in your device’s save.

Pending transactions & refunds

If a purchase awaits Apple’s approval, content stays locked until the transaction is verified. You can play the free puzzles while you wait. For transaction issues or refund requests, use Apple’s refund support. Apple determines refund eligibility under its terms and applicable law; your mandatory consumer rights remain protected.

Saves on your device

Puzzles can be played offline. Progress and preferences stay on your device; there is no game account or automatic cloud sync. Deleting the app with its data or losing the device’s save may result in lost progress. Local backup and recovery options can help when a usable save is available; they cannot recover every loss of data.

Settings → Reset progress starts game progress over and archives the previous save locally for recovery. It does not delete every local file or cancel your Apple purchase. The privacy policy explains stored information, the scope of resetting and deletion options.
